About Me

Scientific Machine Learning Engineer combining mathematical rigor, physical modeling, and scalable software systems.

I am a Scientific Machine Learning & AI Engineer with industrial R&D experience at IFP Energies nouvelles (IFPEN) and graduate of Université Paris-Saclay, where I completed my Master's in Computer & Network Systems (Autonomous Systems track) as the second-ranked student in M2.

At IFPEN, I developed reproducible Python ML pipelines for complex multi-physics engineering prediction, designing physics-guided neural architectures and surrogate models for electric-motor loss maps. My work focused on Feature-wise Linear Modulation (FiLM), physical constraint formulation, and designing rigorous validation protocols to ensure robust zero-shot generalization across unseen motor geometries.

My background bridges applied Deep Learning (PyTorch, Transformers, Computer Vision), Scientific ML (PINNs, surrogate modeling, OOD evaluation), and Systems Engineering (distributed architectures, concurrency in C and Java, Docker, CI/CD).
